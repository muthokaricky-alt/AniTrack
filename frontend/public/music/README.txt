Background music for AniTrack.

The three tracks here are original loops generated for this project, so there are no licensing problems.

To use your own songs: drop the mp3 files in this folder and list them in playlist.json
(title, artist, src). Only use music you own or that is royalty-free / Creative Commons;
popular anime songs are copyrighted and can't be hosted on a public site without permission.
Keep files small (under ~3 MB each) so the site stays fast.
